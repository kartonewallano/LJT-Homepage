## Abstract

This paper investigates the composition of parameter-efficient modules through arithmetic operations. The authors demonstrate that arithmetic operations such as addition and subtraction can be applied to parameter-efficient fine-tuning modules, enabling flexible and efficient model adaptation without full fine-tuning.
