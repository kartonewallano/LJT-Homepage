## Abstract

This paper explores the perception bottleneck of Vision-Language Models (VLMs) when applied to chart understanding. It identifies key limitations in how VLMs perceive and interpret visual chart elements and proposes approaches to address these challenges.

**Code:** Vision4Chart, available on GitHub.
