## Abstract

This paper discovers a universal truthfulness hyperplane inside large language models. Through systematic analysis, the authors identify a linear hyperplane in the representation space of LLMs that universally encodes truthfulness across different models and tasks, providing new insights into LLM interpretability and truthfulness.

**Code:** Universal_Truthfulness_Hyperplane, available on GitHub.
